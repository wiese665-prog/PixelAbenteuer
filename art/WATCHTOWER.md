# Native watchtower

The final `art/source/watchtower-native.png` is a 160x208 native sprite assembled from the original Exterior_house, exterior and Walls_street reference sheets. Original brick, timber, window, door and roof pixels are retained at 1:1 resolution. There is no image-generation illustration or color quantization in the final tower.

`art/source/watchtower-palette.json` lists the exact opaque RGB colors copied from those sheets. Transparency is binary. The masonry pattern uses the source 16px tiles; native windows and the door retain their original size.
